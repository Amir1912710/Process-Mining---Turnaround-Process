# Requirements for the next realism milestone

The v2.1 pilot deliberately holds these features fixed. They must not be silently changed inside an ongoing comparison.

1. **Local-time movement policy.** Resolve the UTC/local mismatch, shoulder windows and exception categories described in `policy_review.md`. Represent movement restrictions separately from cabin service and pre-boarding rules. The disabled-curfew scenario is diagnostic only.
2. **Document the physical scope.** BER's June 2025 [airport facilities factsheet](https://corporate.berlin-airport.de/content/dam/corporate/en/unternehmen-presse/ber/flughafenanlagen-ber-en.pdf) describes north/south runway lengths of 3,600/4,000 metres, 25 Terminal 1 boarding bridges and 105 midfield aircraft parking positions. The inherited example instead has 25 modeled stands and fictional geometry. These categories are not interchangeable: boarding bridges are not the complete stand inventory. Verify the study-period inventory and define whether the model represents an airport-wide system or a restricted stand subset before changing resource ratios.
3. **Ground interval regimes.** Among the input cases, 39/271 on November 1 and 44/238 on November 8 exceed six hours. Separate long stays and operational turnarounds before empirical duration fitting. The present model has no complete departure timetable, planned parking or overnight-stand policy.
4. **Missing service context.** Supported candidate aircraft types exist for 216/271 and 180/238 rows respectively; this is not observed passenger demand. Unknown carrier class, assumed 85% load factor, assumed zero PRM demand and 120-minute sector assumptions remain explicit. Reconcile aircraft-type candidates across rotations before claiming an observed fleet configuration.
5. **One service at a time if attribution is needed.** The current timing comparison changes cleaning, fueling and boarding together. It cannot isolate the effect of each service, or distinguish a change in a distribution's mean from a change in variability. Add separate interventions or mean-matched distributions only under a new fixed experimental design.
6. **Empirical evaluation.** Compare only observable quantities with their matching definitions. Keep simulated AIBT–AOBT separate from OpenSky airborne-boundary proxies. Later-date scenario stability is not, by itself, predictive validation.

7. **Demand coverage and initialization.** The matched core cohort excludes unmatched arrivals/departures and other discarded movements. It does not represent all airport demand competing for resources. Daily runs also start with free stands and resources, without calibrated overnight occupancy. Add complete demand coverage or define a restricted cohort scenario explicitly before drawing airport-wide congestion conclusions.

The current pilot can support conditional sensitivity findings and identify model weaknesses. It cannot establish optimal actual BER staffing or certified operational performance.
